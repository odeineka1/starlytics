CATEGORY ANALYSIS:
**Operational Efficiency:** Customers are increasingly frustrated by "rude," "slow," and "disorganized" service, with specific complaints directed at "waiter" and "manager" interactions, despite some appreciation for "fast," "friendly," and "attentive" staff. The declining score indicates that negative experiences are outweighing positive ones significantly.

**Value Proposition:** While a small segment perceives "value" and "reasonable" pricing, the overwhelming sentiment is that offerings are "overpriced," a "rip-off," and "expensive," leading to a sharp decline in perceived worth.

**Physical Environment:** Although some customers appreciate a "cozy," "clean," and "warm" "atmosphere" with good "seating," there are growing complaints about the "atmosphere" being "cold," "dirty," or "messy," alongside issues with "seating" and "parking."

**Product/Offering:** Despite some praise for "delicious," "fresh," and "quality" "food" and "menu" items, a much larger volume of feedback describes the "food" as "disgusting," "stale," or "burnt," indicating a severe and widespread problem with the core product's "quality."

ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS:
1.  **Mandatory Service Recovery Training:** Immediately implement comprehensive training for all staff, especially "waiter" and "manager" roles, focusing on politeness, attentiveness, and efficiency to combat "rude," "slow," and "ignored" complaints. This must include protocols for handling customer issues to prevent "disinterested" interactions.
2.  **Urgent Food Quality Overhaul:** Conduct an immediate and thorough review of all "food" preparation processes and ingredient sourcing to eliminate "disgusting," "stale," and "burnt" items. Focus on consistent "quality" and "fresh" ingredients to reverse the overwhelming negative sentiment.
3.  **Value Reassessment & Communication:** Analyze pricing against competitors and customer expectations to address the "overpriced," "rip-off," and "expensive" perceptions. Consider introducing new menu items or bundles that clearly demonstrate "value" or enhance the perceived worth to justify current pricing.
4.  **Environmental Cleanliness & Comfort Audit:** Implement daily enhanced cleaning protocols to eliminate "dirty" and "messy" conditions. Address "cold" temperatures and evaluate "seating" arrangements for comfort, while also investigating solutions for "parking" challenges.

STRATEGY SUMMARY:
The business is facing a critical downturn across all customer experience pillars, with declining scores indicating a systemic problem. While there are glimpses of positive experiences, the overwhelming negative feedback, particularly regarding "food" quality and "rude" "operational efficiency," is severely hurting the brand. The single most important thing to fix first is the core "food" product, as its failure ("disgusting," "stale," "burnt") undermines all other efforts.
